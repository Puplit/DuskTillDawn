Players

Everyone on the server right now. Search to reach anyone else.

Tools

Player mode, spawns and announcements — each action checks its own permission

Teleports

The panel closes when you teleport so you can see it happen

Consumables

Food, drinks and medicine for the metabolism — changes push to players instantly; brand-new items appear in inventories after a vorp_inventory restart

Smokes

Pipes, cigars and cigarettes — stored in the smokes table, changes are live; the item itself must exist in vorp_inventory

Deaths

What a respawn took off each player. Restore hands all of it back in one click — the character has to be online. Rows stay after a restore so the history is kept.

Crafting

Recipes for every crafting station — needs the manage_crafting permission. Edits go live when a player re-opens a station. Recipes are DISABLED, never deleted, so player favourites never shift.

Weapon Crafting

Weapon & ammo recipes for the gunsmith — needs the manage_crafting permission. The weapon SHOP is separate and unaffected. Edits go live when a player re-opens a gunsmith.

Housing

Every house and motel room — needs the manage_housing permission. Create House starts the door capture at your location; everything else is managed here.

Society

Businesses, staff, ledgers and shops — needs the manage_society permission. Create businesses and place their points right here; positions use wherever you are standing.

Ranches

Every ranch, its owner, hands, herd and production — needs the manage_ranch permission; price/tax editable, lifecycle stays in-world

Stores

Player-owned shops, their owners and ledgers — needs the manage_stores permission; society shops live under each business in Society

Gangs

Every gang — faction, level, points, roster, territory, wars and live drug plants. Needs the manage_gangs permission. Points drive BOTH the map radius and the perk level, so changing them changes what a gang can do. The season reset table clears every ladder at once and needs reset_gang_points.

Perks

Character progression — level, experience, perk points and everything unlocked. Needs the manage_perks permission. Works on offline characters; an online one sees the change immediately.

Banks

Every character's bank balances and cash, richest first — needs the manage_bank permission. Works on offline characters; every wipe or set is written to the bank ledger under your name.

Curses

Everyone online, and what they carry. Give a curse, ease a harmful one back to its start, or cleanse it. Offline carriers are listed too, and can be cleansed. Needs the manage_curses permission. Changes are logged under your name.

Farms

Every plant in the ground, by the character who planted it. Open a grower to see their plants, go to one, or pull it up. Needs the manage_farms permission. Changes reach players in-game at once and are logged under your name.

Hideouts

Every underground hideout — owner, co-owners, who is inside, ledger, stash, raids and production. Needs the manage_hideouts permission. Changes reach players in-game immediately and are logged under your name.

Camps

Every gang camp — leader, members, safe, tax pot, stash, furniture and repossession. Needs the manage_camps permission. Changes reach players in-game immediately and are logged under your name.

World Props

Props placed permanently into the map with the prop placer. They exist for every player until removed here. Removing one needs the delete_world_prop permission and is logged under your name.

Events

Seasonal events from pup-events. Forcing one on changes the world for everyone, and the clock control moves the whole server's in-game time. Needs the manage_events permission.

Weather

Time, weather and wind for the whole server — needs the manage_weather permission. Everything here is live for every player the moment it is applied. The readouts refresh once a second while this page is open.

Anti-Cheat

Detections are evidence, not proof — spectate before acting

Door Locks

Every registered door — needs the manage_doorlocks permission. Set which jobs hold a key and jump to any door. Changes are live for everyone immediately, no restart.

Server Bans

Every ban carries a Ban ID — that is what the player is told and what they appeal with. Search by ID, name, Discord, Steam, licence, IP or reason.

Items DB

Edits land in the database instantly — restart vorp_inventory to apply in-game

Live Map

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a player for actions, right-click the map to teleport there.
